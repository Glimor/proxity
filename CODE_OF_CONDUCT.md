# Code of Conduct

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This policy applies
to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Assume good intent, accept corrections, and keep discussions relevant.
- Protect personal information, credentials, and private communications.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, doxxing, spam, and
sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer using the project's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/Glimor/proxity/security/advisories/new).
For conduct reports, use the title "Conduct report" and avoid unnecessary
personal information. Do not post the incident publicly. The maintainer will
limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate and respond, but cannot
promise absolute confidentiality or a response deadline.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or temporarily or permanently ban accounts, depending on the
severity and recurrence. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and
applied consistently; maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
